What Parrlo Translate is

Parrlo Translate is a consecutive translation app. It turns your spoken language into a correct sentence in another language and translates the other person’s reply. By installing or using Parrlo you agree to these terms.

Accuracy of translation

Parrlo helps people understand each other but can make mistakes. Don’t rely on the app alone where an error could be dangerous or have legal consequences: medical care, contracts, courts, police, official procedures. In such cases use a professional translator. In an emergency, call 112.
