A short guide to using Parrlo Translate. Can’t find your answer? Write to us.

How to start a conversation

Tap the button on your side (for example, “Me”) and speak in your language. When you finish, Parrlo shows the literary text and the translation. The other person replies with their button — the conversation goes turn by turn.

How to fix a word

Tap the underlined word in the literary text and pick the right option. Tap any line to view the original, copy the text or fix a word.

How to flag an inexact translation

In the conversation summary, inexact spots carry a flag. Tap the flag and briefly write what’s wrong.

The other person doesn’t have Parrlo

Show them the QR code on your screen. They point the camera and the conversation opens in their browser, with no install and no sign-up.

No network

In settings, download the offline translator: Parrlo will then translate right on your phone. If the model isn’t downloaded yet, the app tells you what to do.

No microphone access

Open your phone’s settings, find Parrlo and allow microphone access.

Your voice

You can delete your voice at any time in the app settings or by writing to hello@parrlo.com.

Still have questions?
